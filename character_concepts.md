# Character Concepts - Lingua Quest: The City of Words

## Goal
Provide 3-5 avatar concepts and 3-5 Spirit Companion concepts with short design notes and naming suggestions. These are light design briefs for concept artists.

---

## Avatar Concepts (Player Characters)

1) The Explorer
- Visuals: Light leather coat, satchel, compass necklace, practical boots.
- Personality: Curious, brave, friendly.
- Colors: Earth tones (olive, tan) with teal accent.
- Equipment/Cosmetics: Backpacks, map scrolls, explorer hat.
- Suggested Names: "Ari", "Maya", "Leo"
- Notes: Works well with the "Legendary Expedition" story arc. Avatar animations include walking, inspecting map, planting a flag.

2) The Scholar
- Visuals: Glasses, long coat/cardigan, books strapped to belt, parchment.
- Personality: Thoughtful, patient, encouraging.
- Colors: Deep blues and maroons.
- Equipment/Cosmetics: Bookmark accessory, scrolls, reading lamp.
- Suggested Names: "Evelyn", "Quinn", "Owen"
- Notes: Good for grammar/knowledge themed rewards; library building fits thematically.

3) The Street Artist
- Visuals: Casual hoodie, paint-splattered trousers, headphones, sneakers.
- Personality: Creative, energetic, playful.
- Colors: Bright neons with charcoal base.
- Equipment/Cosmetics: Spray can, sticker packs, skateboard.
- Suggested Names: "Rin", "Kai", "Zoe"
- Notes: Matches Word Arena and creative mini-games; ideal for cosmetics and limited-time skins.

4) The Traveler (bonus)
- Visuals: Light traveler's coat, scarf, camera, sunhat.
- Personality: Open-minded, adventurous.
- Colors: Pastel palette with warm highlights.
- Suggested Names: "Sam", "Lina"

5) The Gadgeteer (bonus)
- Visuals: Futuristic goggles, tool belt with language gadgets, small drone.
- Personality: Inventive, clever.
- Colors: Silver, cyan, black.
- Suggested Names: "Nova", "Dex"

## Spirit Companion Concepts

1) The Little Owl
- Visuals: Small, round owl with oversized eyes and a book-shaped feather patch.
- Personality: Wise, supportive, gentle.
- Function: Offers grammar tips, cheer animations on success.
- Suggested Names: "Blinky", "Atlas"

2) The Pocket Fox
- Visuals: Tiny fox with a glowing tail and a satchel.
- Personality: Playful, mischievous, encouraging.
- Function: Provides hints, collects daily reward tokens.
- Suggested Names: "Flick", "Miso"

3) The Lantern Bug
- Visuals: A cute beetle with a small lantern; light changes color on streaks.
- Personality: Cheerful, persistent.
- Function: Highlights words in Memory Vault; acts as a progress meter.
- Suggested Names: "Glim", "Lumi"

4) The Tiny Robot
- Visuals: Retro-futuristic mini-robot with a screen face that shows emojis.
- Personality: Helpful, witty.
- Function: Shows pronunciation tips, plays short audio samples.
- Suggested Names: "Chip", "Byte"

5) The Cloud Puff
- Visuals: A fluffy cloud-like creature with tiny eyes and a speech-bubble tail.
- Personality: Dreamy, calming.
- Function: Provides daily motivation messages; soothes players after mistakes.
- Suggested Names: "Puff", "Nimbus"

---

## Cosmetic & Progression Notes
- Each avatar and companion has unlockable cosmetics purchasable with Gold or Gems.
- Companions can have small, upgradeable utility (e.g., +5% Memory Vault XP) to avoid pay-to-win: cosmetics and tiny bonuses only.
- Offer starter pack: one default avatar + one default companion; extras through Legendary Expedition rewards or store purchases.

---

## Deliverables for Concept Artist
- 3 full-body colored sheets for Avatars (front, 3/4, back)
- 3 expression sheets for each avatar (neutral, happy, surprised)
- 3 small pose sprites of companions (idle, cheer, hint)
- A short naming and lore paragraph per design (2-3 lines)
